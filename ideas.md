# NodexPlatform compact UI direction

## Theme

**Quiet control room** — a focused platform for people, workspaces, and requests rather than a decorative marketing site.

## Design system

- **Movement:** compact product editorial.
- **Principles:** clear hierarchy, low visual noise, short paths to action, restrained depth.
- **Colors:** navy ink `#14213d`, cobalt `#2355d6`, teal `#18a999`, pale blue surfaces, slate text.
- **Layout:** 1120px content frame, 12px cards, 60px navigation, generous section rhythm but compact controls.
- **Signature element:** small cobalt/teal status chips and icon tiles.
- **Interaction:** immediate selection states, short success/error alerts, same-origin session APIs.
- **Animation:** subtle existing reveal animations only; no large motion or distracting gradients.
- **Typography:** system sans stack, 15px base, responsive display sizes, compact labels.
- **Brand voice:** direct, warm, practical, confident.
- **Wordmark:** `NodexPlatform` with a small lightning mark.
- **Signature color:** cobalt `#2355d6`.
